2. Capacitor Formula

C = ε₀ εᵣ A / d

As overlap area A increases, capacitance increases. These ideal calculations assume air as the dielectric, maximum area 100 cm² and plate separation 1 mm.

4. Working Principle

1. Two conductive plates form a capacitor.
2. One plate slides horizontally.
3. The overlapping area changes with the movement.
4. Greater overlap produces greater capacitance.
5. A measuring circuit converts capacitance changes into an electrical signal related to displacement.
