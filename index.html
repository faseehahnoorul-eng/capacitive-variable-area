2. Capacitor Formula

C = ε₀ εᵣ A / d

As the overlapping area A increases, capacitance increases. These ideal values assume air as the dielectric, a maximum overlap area of 100 cm², and a plate separation of 1 mm.

4. Working Principle

1. Two conductive plates form a capacitor.
2. One plate slides horizontally.
3. The overlapping area changes with the movement.
4. Greater overlap produces greater capacitance.
5. A measuring circuit can convert the capacitance change into a displacement-related electrical signal.
